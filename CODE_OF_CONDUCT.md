# Code of Conduct

Orbitl discussions and contributions should be useful, direct, and respectful. Critique code, evidence, and decisions without attacking people. Make room for different experience levels, correct mistakes plainly, and keep private or sensitive material out of public conversations.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate exposure of private information, impersonation, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Follow maintainer requests about removing sensitive data or moving a discussion to a private channel.

Maintainers may request changes, close discussions, remove contributions, or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with these expectations. They may consider context and the safety of participants when deciding how to moderate a discussion.

If a conduct concern needs private handling, use an existing private repository contact route and avoid publishing sensitive details. Keep reports factual and include the relevant discussion or contribution when it is safe to do so.
